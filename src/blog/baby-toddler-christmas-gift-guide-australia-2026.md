*Last updated 2 November 2026.*

Choosing a baby or toddler Christmas gift in Australia is easier when you know the age, the budget and the safety basics before you start shopping. This guide covers gift ideas by age, what to check before you buy, and the sale dates worth planning around, so you can buy early and avoid last-minute panic.

<h2 id="dates">Key dates for Christmas 2026</h2>

| What | Date |
|---|---|
| Black Friday | Friday 27 November 2026 |
| Cyber Monday | Monday 30 November 2026 |
| Christmas Day | Friday 25 December 2026 |
| Boxing Day | Saturday 26 December 2026 |

Retailers set their own last order dates for Christmas delivery, so check each store's cut-off before you buy. Our [Black Friday baby deals guide](/blog/black-friday-baby-deals-australia-2026/) explains how to prepare for the November sales.

<h2 id="before-you-buy">Before you buy: three quick checks</h2>

1. **Age.** A gift that is right for a 3-year-old can be unsafe for a 9-month-old. Follow the age label on the box.
2. **What they already have.** Ask the parents for a short list. Babies and toddlers outgrow things quickly, and storage is limited.
3. **Where it fits.** Large gifts, such as ride-ons or play kitchens, need space. A smaller gift may be better for a small home.

<h2 id="newborn">Gift ideas for newborns and babies up to 6 months</h2>

Newborns don't need much. The best gifts are often for the parents.

- **Soft books and cloth books** with high-contrast pictures.
- **Muslin wraps and swaddles** in natural fabrics.
- **A play gym or play mat** for tummy time.
- **Soft rattles and teething toys** that are made for babies.
- **Practical gifts for parents**, such as a meal delivery voucher or a cleaning service voucher.

<h2 id="six-to-twelve">Gift ideas for 6 to 12 months</h2>

At this age babies start to sit, crawl and grab.

- **Stacking cups and nesting toys.**
- **Board books** with flaps or textures.
- **Bath toys** that dry easily.
- **Simple musical toys** with a volume control.
- **Soft blocks** that are too big to swallow.

<h2 id="one-to-two">Gift ideas for 1 to 2 years</h2>

Toddlers love moving, building and copying what adults do.

- **Push-along walkers** that are stable and not too fast.
- **Chunky puzzles** with large pieces.
- **Wooden or soft building blocks.**
- **Pretend play sets**, such as a toy kitchen or tea set.
- **Ride-on toys** for indoors or the backyard. A helmet is a good add-on.

<h2 id="two-to-three">Gift ideas for 2 to 3 years</h2>

- **Art supplies** such as chunky crayons and washable paint.
- **Simple board games** made for young children.
- **Dress-up clothes** and toy tools.
- **Picture books** they can read again and again.
- **Outdoor toys** such as a sandpit set, bubbles or a small ball.

<h2 id="safety">Safety checks before you buy</h2>

A cheap price isn't worth it if the toy is unsafe.

- **Small parts.** Toys for children under 3 shouldn't have small parts that can fit in the mouth.
- **Button batteries.** These can be very dangerous if swallowed. Check that the battery compartment is secured with a screw.
- **Magnets.** Small, high-powered magnets can cause serious injury if swallowed. Keep them away from young children.
- **Cords and strings.** Avoid long strings or cords on cots and toys.
- **Cheap overseas sellers.** Be careful with unknown marketplace sellers for children's products, and check for recalls on [Product Safety Australia](https://www.productsafety.gov.au).

<h2 id="budget">How to stick to a budget</h2>

- **Decide a limit per child** before you start.
- **Pool gifts with family** for one bigger item, such as a ride-on.
- **Track prices now.** Note today's price for the gifts you like. If the price during Black Friday isn't lower, skip it.
- **Don't buy something you can't return.** Check the return policy and keep the receipt.

<h2 id="checklist">Gift shopping checklist</h2>

- [ ] Age and size noted for each child
- [ ] Wish list from the parents
- [ ] Budget set for each gift
- [ ] Prices noted at two or three retailers
- [ ] Safety label and age grading checked
- [ ] Return policy and delivery cut-off dates checked
- [ ] Gifts bought before the delivery cut-off

<h2 id="faq">Christmas gift guide FAQ</h2>

### What is a good Christmas gift for a 1-year-old?
Chunky puzzles, stacking toys, board books and push-along walkers are all popular. Choose something simple that matches their age.

### What should I buy for a newborn?
Practical items such as wraps, soft books and a play mat. A gift for the parents, such as a meal voucher, is often the most useful.

### When should I buy Christmas gifts for toddlers?
Early. Track prices now, and compare them with Black Friday on 27 November. Don't wait until the last week, when stock and delivery slots run out.

### Are Black Friday toys cheaper?
Sometimes. Check prices before the sale, because a "was" price isn't always real.

### Is it safe to buy children's toys online?
Yes, if you buy from reputable retailers and check the age label and recalls. Be careful with unknown overseas sellers.
