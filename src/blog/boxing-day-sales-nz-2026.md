Boxing Day in New Zealand isn't quite the same event it is in Australia — smaller retailer
participation, but genuinely deep discounts where it does happen.

## Where to look first

- **Farmers and Briscoes** typically run their biggest markdowns of the year
- **The Warehouse** clears seasonal stock hard in the days after Christmas
- Online-only deals often start a day earlier than in-store

## What to skip

"Up to 70% off" signage is almost always on a small clearance rack, not the whole store —
check a few specific items you actually want before heading out.
