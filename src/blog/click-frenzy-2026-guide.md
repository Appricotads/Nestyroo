Click Frenzy is Australia's answer to Black Friday — but timing and retailer participation shift every year, so a bit of prep goes a long way.

## How to shop it well

1. Build a wishlist a week ahead so you know real prices, not sale-tagged prices
2. Set alerts for the categories you actually need, not everything
3. Shop early in the sale window — best stock goes first
