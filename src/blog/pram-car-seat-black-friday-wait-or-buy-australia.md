*Last updated 13 November 2026.*

Is now a good time to buy a pram or car seat, or should you wait for Black Friday? The short answer: if bub is due soon, buy now. If you have a few months, track prices and wait for the sales, as long as the discount is real. This guide helps you decide, based on your due date, what's already been discounted and how to check a deal.

<h2 id="decide">Should you buy now or wait?</h2>

The most important question is how long until you need it.

| Your situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| Due in the next 4 to 6 weeks | Buy now. A car seat must be fitted before you leave hospital, and a late delivery isn't worth the risk. |
| Due in 2 to 3 months | Track prices now, set a target, and decide after Black Friday and Cyber Monday. Leave time for delivery and fitting. |
| Due in 4 or more months | You have time. Watch prices through Black Friday, Boxing Day and the sales after. |
| Already have bub and need an upgrade | Wait for a good discount, unless the current one is unsafe or doesn't fit. |

If you're unsure, buy the car seat first, because it's the one you can't leave for later. A pram can sometimes be borrowed or bought quickly.

<h2 id="do-they-go-on-sale">Do prams and car seats go on sale for Black Friday?</h2>

Often, but not always, and not every model. Sale calendars regularly point to big-ticket household and baby items in November, and retailers use these events to clear stock. But:

- **Not every brand or model is discounted.** Popular models may not be.
- **Retailers announce deals late.** Many publish prices only days before the sale starts, so you may not know in advance.
- **Stock can run out.** The model you want may sell out early.
- **Some "sale" prices are only slightly lower** than the usual price.

That's why the price check below matters more than the date.

<h2 id="price-check">How to check that a pram or car seat deal is real</h2>

1. **Record today's price** at two or three retailers, with the date. A screenshot works.
2. **Note the exact model name and number.** Some sale versions are slightly different.
3. **Set a target price** you'd be happy to pay.
4. **Check what's included.** A bundle with a capsule, adapters or a rain cover may be worth more, or less, than it looks.
5. **Add delivery, fitting and returns.** A lower headline price can disappear once you add costs.
6. **Check the safety details.** Child car seats must meet the standard, and prams and strollers have a mandatory safety standard. Check [Product Safety Australia](https://www.productsafety.gov.au) for recalls.

If the price doesn't reach your target, wait. Another sale will come, and a good pram or car seat is rarely only available once.

<h2 id="car-seat-first">Why the car seat comes first</h2>

You can't take your baby home in a car without a properly fitted, approved restraint. So:

- Buy the car seat before your due date, ideally with a few weeks to spare.
- Practise fitting it, and consider having it checked by a qualified fitter.
- Don't buy a second-hand seat unless you know its full history.

Our [car seat buying guide](/blog/car-seat-buying-guide-australia/) covers the types, the rules and what to check.

<h2 id="pram">Pram timing</h2>

A pram is less urgent than a car seat, because you can carry or wear your baby for the first few weeks. That gives you more freedom to wait for a good price. Do test a pram in a shop before you buy, so you know it suits you and your car. Our [pram buying guide](/blog/pram-buying-guide-australia/) explains what to look for.

<h2 id="faq">FAQ</h2>

### Is now a good time to buy a pram in Australia?
If you need it soon, yes. If you have a few months, record prices now and see what's offered on Black Friday, Cyber Monday and Boxing Day.

### Do car seats go on sale for Black Friday?
Some do, but there's no guarantee any specific model will. Retailers often announce deals late, so set a target price and check.

### When is the best time to buy a pram?
Usually in the last few months of pregnancy. Big sales like Black Friday, Boxing Day and EOFY can lower the price, if the discount is real.

### Should I wait for Boxing Day instead?
If you have time and Black Friday doesn't reach your target price, yes. Boxing Day is a second chance, but stock can be limited.

### Is it safe to buy a pram or car seat online?
Yes, from a reputable seller that states it meets the Australian standard. Be careful with unfamiliar sellers with prices far below everyone else's.
