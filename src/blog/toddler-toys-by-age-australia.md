Toddlers change fast, and a toy that's perfect at 12 months can be boring or even unsafe a year later. This guide shows what toys suit toddlers at 1, 2 and 3 years old, what each type helps with, what to check on the label, and how to buy without filling the house with plastic.

<h2 id="how-toddlers-play">How toddlers play</h2>

Toddlers learn by touching, stacking, pouring, pushing and copying you. The best toys are simple, so your child decides how to use them. Most toddler toys fall into a few types:

| Type of play | What it helps with | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Moving | Balance, strength, coordination | Push-along walkers, ride-ons, balls |
| Hands and fingers | Fine motor skills | Stacking cups, chunky puzzles, blocks |
| Pretend play | Imagination and language | Toy kitchen, tea set, dolls |
| Books and music | Language and listening | Board books, simple instruments |
| Making things | Creativity | Chunky crayons, washable paint, play dough |
| Outdoors | Energy and exploring | Sandpit set, bubbles, a small ball |

<h2 id="age-1">Toys for 1 year olds</h2>

At 12 months, most toddlers are crawling, cruising or taking first steps. They love putting things in and taking them out.

- **Push-along walker:** choose one that is stable and doesn't tip.
- **Stacking and nesting cups:** also fun in the bath.
- **Chunky puzzles** with a few large pieces.
- **Board books** with flaps and textures.
- **Large soft blocks** that are too big to swallow.
- **Simple musical toys** such as a shaker or drum.

<h2 id="age-2">Toys for 2 year olds</h2>

Two year olds run, climb, copy and say a lot more. They want to do what you do.

- **Pretend play sets:** a toy kitchen, tea set or doctor kit.
- **Ride-on toys** for the backyard or park, with a helmet if it has wheels.
- **Wooden or interlocking blocks.**
- **Puzzles** with a few more pieces.
- **Chunky crayons and washable paint.**
- **Play dough** and safe tools. Supervise closely.
- **Picture books** they can ask for again and again.

<h2 id="age-3">Toys for 3 year olds</h2>

Three year olds start to share, take turns and enjoy stories and rules.

- **Simple board and matching games** made for young children.
- **Dress-up clothes** and costumes.
- **Balance bikes or scooters,** with a helmet.
- **Building sets** with larger pieces.
- **Art supplies** and stickers.
- **Outdoor toys** such as a cubby house, sandpit toys or a water table.

<h2 id="safety">Safety checks for toddler toys</h2>

- **Read the age label.** If it says not suitable for children under 3, keep it away from toddlers.
- **Small parts.** Anything that fits through a toilet paper tube can be a choking hazard.
- **Button batteries.** The battery compartment should be secured with a screw. A swallowed button battery needs urgent medical help.
- **Magnets.** Small, powerful magnets can cause serious injury if swallowed.
- **Cords and strings.** Avoid long strings on toys, and keep them away from cots.
- **Check for damage.** Throw out toys with cracks, sharp edges or loose parts.
- **Check for recalls** on [Product Safety Australia](https://www.productsafety.gov.au), especially for second-hand toys.

<h2 id="buying">How to buy toddler toys without overspending</h2>

1. **Choose fewer, simpler toys.** A handful of open-ended toys gets used more than a pile of gadgets.
2. **Rotate toys.** Keep some in a box and swap them every few weeks. They feel new again.
3. **Try a toy library or borrow.** Many councils and community groups lend toys.
4. **Buy second-hand for large toys.** Check for recalls, loose parts and damage first.
5. **Track prices.** Note the price at two or three retailers, and compare during sales. Black Friday is on 27 November 2026, Cyber Monday is on 30 November and Boxing Day is on 26 December.
6. **Ask family for what you need.** A short list stops duplicates.

<h2 id="faq">Toddler toys FAQ</h2>

### What toys are good for a 1 year old?
Push-along walkers, stacking cups, chunky puzzles, board books and large soft blocks are all popular. Choose simple toys with no small parts.

### What are good toys for a 2 year old?
Pretend play sets, blocks, ride-ons, chunky crayons, play dough and picture books.

### How many toys does a toddler need?
Fewer than you'd think. A small set of simple toys that you rotate is usually enough.

### Are second-hand toys safe?
Often, if they're in good condition, have no loose or small parts, and haven't been recalled. Wash them before use.

### When should I buy toddler toys on sale?
Black Friday, Cyber Monday and Boxing Day are common sale times, but compare against your recorded prices first.
