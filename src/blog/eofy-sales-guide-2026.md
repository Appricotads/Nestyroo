The EOFY sales guide for Australian households starts with one date: the financial year ends on **Wednesday 30 June 2027**, and retailers use it for some of the biggest sales of the year. If you plan ahead, you can pick up appliances, TVs, laptops, furniture and family essentials for less, without buying something you don't need in a rush.

This guide explains when EOFY sales usually start, what is commonly discounted, how to check a deal is real, and how to prepare months ahead. *Last updated 22 October 2026.*

<h2 id="what-is-eofy">What is EOFY and why are there sales?</h2>

EOFY stands for end of financial year. In Australia the financial year runs from 1 July to 30 June. Retailers and brands run sales around the end of June to clear stock, hit annual targets and tap into shoppers who are thinking about tax time.

Some businesses promote items as tax deductions. Be careful with that angle. Whether you can claim something depends on your own circumstances, so check with the ATO or a registered tax agent rather than relying on a retailer's claim.

<h2 id="dates">When do EOFY sales start?</h2>

| What | Typical timing |
|---|---|
| Early EOFY deals | From May, with some retailers starting in the first weeks of the month |
| Main EOFY sales | Early to mid June |
| EOFY ends | Wednesday 30 June 2027 |
| Extended sales | Some run into the first week of July |

These are typical patterns, not confirmed 2027 dates. Individual sales can run from a few days to several weeks, and major brands often run them for up to a month. We'll update this guide once retailers announce their 2027 sales.

<h2 id="what-to-buy">What is commonly discounted at EOFY</h2>

- **Tech and appliances:** laptops, TVs, printers, phones, washing machines and fridges are some of the most promoted EOFY categories.
- **Furniture and homewares:** mattresses, sofas, bedding and kitchen items often get seasonal discounts.
- **Family and baby gear:** prams, car seats, cots and high chairs are sometimes included. See our [baby gear and nursery guides](/category/baby-gear-nursery/).
- **Toys and kids' items:** useful for planning ahead for birthdays and Christmas.
- **Clothing and shoes:** end-of-season winter stock is often reduced in June.

Sales are rarely store-wide, and the discounted products can change from day to day.

<h2 id="before-june">How to prepare before June</h2>

1. **Make a needs list.** Write down what you actually plan to replace in the next 12 months, such as the washing machine, a TV or a laptop.
2. **Track prices now.** Note today's price at two or three retailers. A price you recorded earlier is the best proof of a real discount.
3. **Set a budget.** Decide a maximum for each item before you see the sale price.
4. **Read reviews.** Some discounted models are cheap because they perform poorly.
5. **Join retailer emails and loyalty programs.** Some deals are for members only or online only.
6. **Check price matching.** Ask whether a retailer will match a lower price elsewhere.

<h2 id="fake-discounts">How to spot a fake EOFY discount</h2>

- The "was" price is higher than the item has really sold for.
- The "sale" price is the same as the price a month ago.
- A countdown timer pushes you to buy without comparing.
- The model is a special "sale version" you can't compare with anything else.
- The discount applies only to a version with fewer features.

If the price isn't lower than the best recent price, skip it. Good deals appear throughout the year, not only in June. Our [Black Friday guide](/blog/black-friday-baby-deals-australia-2026/) and [Click Frenzy guide](/blog/click-frenzy-2026-baby-deals-guide/) cover the big sales that come first.

<h2 id="safety">Safe shopping tips</h2>

- Buy from retailers you know, and check the web address carefully. Fake copies of well-known stores appear around big sales.
- Be careful with cheap overseas sellers for children's products, baby safety items and batteries. Check [Product Safety Australia](https://www.productsafety.gov.au) for recalls.
- Be sceptical of extended warranties. Your rights under the Australian Consumer Law may already cover faults.
- Keep your receipts and read the return policy before you buy.

<h2 id="faq">EOFY sales FAQ</h2>

### When do EOFY sales start in Australia?
Some retailers start in May, but most of the main sales run from early to mid June until 30 June. Some extend into early July.

### When is the end of financial year in Australia?
30 June each year. In 2027 it falls on a Wednesday.

### Are EOFY sales worth it?
They can be, if the discount is genuine and you needed the item anyway. Compare against recent prices rather than the "was" price.

### Are EOFY purchases tax deductible?
Only if the item is used for work or business and you meet the ATO rules. A retailer saying so isn't enough. Check with the ATO or a registered tax agent.

### Is EOFY a good time to buy baby gear?
Sometimes. Compare with Click Frenzy and Black Friday prices, and check Australian safety standards for cots, car seats and prams.

### Should I wait for Black Friday instead?
For some products, yes. Track prices through the year and buy when it hits your target price.
