End of financial year sales run through June, and while retailers push everything, only a handful of categories are genuinely discounted enough to be worth your attention.

## What's actually worth buying

- **Appliances and electronics** — retailers clear stock ahead of new financial year ranges
- **Home office gear** — especially useful if you're claiming a work-from-home deduction
- **Mattresses and furniture** — EOFY is historically one of the two best times of year to buy

## What to skip

Clothing and "everything must go" storewide sales are rarely genuine EOFY discounts — check price history before assuming it's a deal.
