<h2 id="how-it-works">How Click Frenzy works</h2>

Click Frenzy is an Australian online sale event that started in 2012. It isn't a shop. It's a referral and comparison service that lists offers from participating retailers, and you buy from the retailer directly. It also has a Baby & Kids category and a Toys category, which is where most family deals appear.

- **Offers are revealed when the event opens**, so you can't see every price beforehand.
- **A free sign-up gets you early access** and exclusive codes. Use an email you actually check.
- **Stock is limited.** Popular prams, car seats and cots can sell out quickly.
- **Retailer terms apply.** Returns, warranty and delivery are set by each retailer.

<h2 id="prepare">How to prepare for the next big sale</h2>

1. **Make a short list.** Write down the 3 to 5 items you actually need, with a budget for each.
2. **Record today's prices.** Note the current price at two or three retailers now, so you can tell a real discount from a marked-up "was" price.
3. **Check size and fit.** Measure your car boot and back seat before buying a pram or car seat.
4. **Sign up for early access** and save your delivery details with the retailers you trust.
5. **Check return policies.** Know what happens if the item arrives damaged or doesn't fit.

<h2 id="worth-buying">Baby gear that is often worth buying in a big sale</h2>

We don't list prices here until deals are live, because they change fast. These are the categories where parents commonly look for savings:

- **Prams and strollers:** big-ticket items, so a percentage off adds up. Compare against the retailer's usual price.
- **Car seats and capsules:** buy only from an Australian retailer, and check the seat meets the Australian standard.
- **Cots and bassinets:** check the model carefully (see safety below).
- **Nappies and wipes:** worth it only if the per-nappy price is genuinely lower than your usual buy. Work it out per nappy.
- **Baby monitors, carriers and high chairs:** often discounted, but check reviews first.
- **Toys and clothing for older kids:** good for early Christmas shopping.

<h2 id="safety">Safety checks before you buy</h2>

A cheap price doesn't matter if the product isn't safe.

- Child car restraints, cots (including portable cots) and prams and strollers must meet mandatory Australian safety standards. Look for compliance information on the product page.
- Be careful with overseas marketplace sellers who don't state Australian compliance.
- Check [Product Safety Australia](https://www.productsafety.gov.au) for recalls before buying, especially secondhand.
- Never buy a second-hand car seat if you don't know its history.

<h2 id="fake-discounts">How to spot fake discounts</h2>

- The "was" price is higher than the product has ever sold for.
- The "sale" price is the same as last month's price.
- A countdown timer pressures you to buy without comparing.
- A brand-new "bundle" makes it hard to compare against the single item.

Use a price history tool or your own notes from before the sale. If the price isn't lower than the best recent price, skip it. If you missed Click Frenzy, Black Friday is the next big sale: see our [Black Friday baby deals guide](/blog/black-friday-baby-deals-australia-2026/).

<h2 id="faq">Click Frenzy 2026 FAQ</h2>

### When was Click Frenzy 2026?
The Main Event ran from Tuesday 27 October to Friday 30 October 2026.

### Is Click Frenzy better than Black Friday for baby gear?
Not always. Click Frenzy is earlier, so it suits things you need soon. Black Friday has more retailers taking part. Compare prices across both, and don't assume one is always cheaper.

### Is Click Frenzy a store?
No. It's a referral and comparison service. You buy from the participating retailer, and their return and warranty terms apply.

### Do I need to sign up?
You don't have to, but a free sign-up gives early access and exclusive codes.

### Are prams and car seats worth buying in the sale?
They can be, if the discount is real and the product meets Australian safety standards. Compare against the usual price first.

### Can I return baby gear bought in a sale?
Sale items usually have the same consumer guarantees as regular items under Australian Consumer Law, but some retailers set their own change-of-mind rules. Read the policy before you buy.
