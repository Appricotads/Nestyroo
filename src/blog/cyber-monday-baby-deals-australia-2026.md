<h2 id="what">What is Cyber Monday?</h2>

Cyber Monday is the Monday after Black Friday, and it started as an online sale. Many Australian retailers now run one combined Black Friday to Cyber Monday sale, so the offers often end on Cyber Monday night.

<h2 id="check">What to check before you buy</h2>

- **Is the discount real?** Check the price at two or three retailers over the past few weeks. A "50% off" tag only means something if the old price was real. Our guide to [saving money on baby gear](/blog/save-money-on-baby-gear-australia/) explains how.
- **Delivery times.** If you're buying gifts, check the delivery estimate and the last order date before Christmas.
- **Returns and refunds.** Check the retailer's returns policy for sale items, because some offers have exclusions.
- **Stock and rainchecks.** Many offers say "while stocks last" with no rainchecks.
- **The total cost.** Add delivery, and any membership or extra fees, to the price.

<h2 id="safety">Safety checks for baby gear</h2>

A sale price doesn't make an unsafe product safe. Before you buy:

- **Car seats:** check the seat meets AS/NZS 1754 and suits your child's age and size. See our [car seat buying guide](/blog/car-seat-buying-guide-australia/).
- **Cots:** check the cot meets the Australian standard. See our [cot buying guide](/blog/cot-buying-guide-australia/).
- **Everything:** search the product on [Product Safety Australia](https://www.productsafety.gov.au/) for recalls.

Big W's offers run until 9 December, and some retailers keep a smaller sale going into December. Boxing Day (Saturday 26 December) is the next big sales day, and we'll update our guides when retailers publish dates.

<h2 id="faq">Frequently asked questions</h2>

### When is Cyber Monday 2026 in Australia?
Monday 30 November 2026.

### Is Cyber Monday better than Black Friday for baby gear?
Not necessarily. Many Australian retailers run the same sale across the whole weekend, so the offers are often the same. Check the price on the day.

### When do Cyber Monday sales end?
It depends on the retailer. Target's offers mostly end at 11:59pm AEDT on 30 November, and Myer's finishes on Cyber Monday. Check each retailer's own page.

### Will there be more baby sales after Cyber Monday?
Probably. Big W's offers run until 9 December, and Boxing Day is the next major sales event. Retailers may also run smaller December promotions.

### Should I buy a pram or car seat on Cyber Monday?
Only if you've researched the model, checked that it meets the Australian standard and suits your needs, and the price is genuinely lower than usual.
